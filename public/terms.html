Private Hub terms of use

Private Hub is a private, non-commercial personal information dashboard intended solely for its authorised owner.

Read-only banking

Bank connections provide account information such as balances, transactions and connection status. Private Hub does not initiate payments, transfers, beneficiaries, standing orders or any other money movement.

Availability

Bank data may be delayed or temporarily unavailable because of consent expiry, institution availability or service limits. Displayed information is informational and should be confirmed with the relevant financial institution.

Authorised access

Use is limited to the authorised owner. The owner is responsible for protecting access to their email account and for completing bank authentication directly with the institution.
